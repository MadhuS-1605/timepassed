# Privacy Policy for TimePassed

**Last updated: January 20, 2026**

This Privacy Policy describes how TimePassed ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile application (the "App").

## 1. Information Collection and Use

**TimePassed is designed to be privacy-first.**

- **Personal Data**: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number to our servers.
- **Local Storage**: All data regarding your milestones, life events, and preferences is stored locally on your device.
- **Usage Data**: We may collect anonymous, aggregated usage data (such as app crashes or feature usage) to help us improve the App's performance and stability. This data is not linked to your personal identity.

## 2. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:

- **Storage/Photos**: Required only if you choose to upload a photo for your profile or milestones. These images remain on your device and are not uploaded to our servers.
- **Notifications**: Required to send you reminders or alerts that you have scheduled within the App.

## 3. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services that collect information used to identify you. The privacy policies of third-party service providers used by the App include:

- **Google Play Services**: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 5. Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we strive to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. But remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
